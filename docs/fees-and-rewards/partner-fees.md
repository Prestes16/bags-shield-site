# Partner Fees

Transparent fee structure for swap operations.

## Fee Structure

- **Default**: 10 bps (0.1%)
- **Premium**: 20 bps (0.2%)
- **Opt-out**: 0 bps (free)

## When Fees Apply

Fees are only collected when swaps involve SOL/WSOL pairs.

## Fee Accounts

Fees are collected to designated fee accounts based on the output token mint.
