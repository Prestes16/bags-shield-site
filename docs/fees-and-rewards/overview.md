# Fees & Rewards Overview

Bags Shield uses transparent fee structures and reward programs.

## Partner Fees

When using our swap service, a small platform fee may apply:

- **Default**: 10 bps (0.1%)
- **Premium**: 20 bps (0.2%)
- **Opt-out**: 0 bps (free)

Fees are collected only when swaps involve SOL/WSOL pairs.

## Cashback Program

Earn rewards based on your token's Shield Score:

- **Grade A**: 0.6% cashback
- **Grade B**: 0.5% cashback
- **Grade C**: 0.35% cashback
- **Grade D**: 0.2% cashback
- **Grade E**: 0.1% cashback

## Revenue Split

- Treasury: 55%
- Operations: 20%
- Payroll: 15%
- Community Rewards: 10%
