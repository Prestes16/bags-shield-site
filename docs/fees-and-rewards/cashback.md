# Cashback Program

Earn rewards based on your token's Shield Score.

## Cashback Rates

- **Grade A**: 0.6% cashback
- **Grade B**: 0.5% cashback
- **Grade C**: 0.35% cashback
- **Grade D**: 0.2% cashback
- **Grade E**: 0.1% cashback

## How It Works

1. Complete swaps through Bags Shield
2. Earn cashback based on token grade
3. Rewards are credited automatically
4. View balance in your dashboard

## Limits

- Maximum $50 USD per user per day
- Credits expire after 90 days
